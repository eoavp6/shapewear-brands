## FAQ

**What's the difference between light, moderate, and firm compression shapewear?**

Light compression provides gentle smoothing perfect for everyday wear under casual clothes—you'll barely notice you're wearing it. Moderate compression offers noticeable shaping for fitted outfits and work clothes, striking a balance between comfort and visible results. Firm compression delivers maximum smoothing for special occasions and body-conscious dresses, though it typically feels more restrictive and works best for shorter wear periods.

**How do I choose the right shapewear size?**

Always consult the specific brand's size chart rather than assuming your regular clothing size—shapewear sizing varies significantly between manufacturers and running too small will be unbearably uncomfortable while too large won't provide effective shaping. Most brands recommend measuring your natural waist, hips, and torso length, then comparing those measurements to their charts. When in doubt, size up for comfort, especially if you're between sizes.

**Can I wear shapewear every day or is it unhealthy?**

Modern shapewear designed for everyday wear with breathable fabrics and appropriate compression levels is generally safe for daily use. However, avoid wearing firm compression pieces for extended periods, take breaks throughout the day if possible, and never wear shapewear that restricts breathing, causes pain, or leaves deep marks on your skin. Light to moderate compression pieces are better suited for regular, all-day wear.
